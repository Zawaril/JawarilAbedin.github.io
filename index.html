ABOUT

From data to reliable solutions.

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, data engineering, and quantitative analysis. I enjoy turning complex, messy, or high-dimensional data into reliable models, reproducible workflows, and practical solutions.

My experience spans biomedical data analysis, optimization, software engineering, and statistical modelling. I am currently working on automated flow cytometry analysis, where I build end-to-end data pipelines and machine learning workflows. Previously, I developed recurrent-neural-network-based optimization methods for combinatorial problems, which resulted in a peer-reviewed journal publication.

Alongside technical work, I have several years of teaching and mentoring experience in computer science and AI. This has strengthened my ability to communicate complex ideas clearly, collaborate across disciplines, and approach problems from both technical and practical perspectives.

I am interested in opportunities where data, AI, and software can be used to solve meaningful real-world problems, whether through research, product development, or applied engineering.
